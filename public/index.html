Here's What Your GMB Audit Includes

01

Your Business Profile Optimization Score & What To Fix

You get a 0–100% score showing how well your profile is set up to rank online plus a clear Pass/Fail card for each of the 10 key GBP factors Google weighs most.

  • Business description keyword optimization
  • Review frequency & Google rating
  • Review reply rate
  • Number of images uploaded
  • Post frequency & post keyword optimization
  • Services list & service descriptions
  • Service area setup
02

Your Local Market Revenue Opportunity

Your audit estimates the total monthly revenue being spent on businesses in your category and location, based on local search volume and average customer value. An actual dollar estimate that shows what's at stake.

  • Your estimated monthly local search volume
  • Estimated average customer value in your market
  • Estimated total amount spent with your competitors
03

Your Top 3 Competitors on Google Maps

See the 3 businesses currently outranking you in the Google Local Pack — their name, address, star rating, review count, and location on a map. Knowing who you're up against is the first step to overtaking them.

  • Name and address of each top-ranked Google competitor
  • Their star rating and total review count
  • Better understand what local searchers see
04

AI Visibility Check Across All Major LLMs

Your audit searches for your business across 6 major AI platforms and shows whether you're being recommended when someone asks for the best business in your category and city.

  • Checked across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Llama & Perplexity
  • Searched using your actual business category and location
  • Clear Found / Not Found result for each platform
05

Keyword Ranking Heatmaps

For each keyword you select, you get a geographic heatmap showing where you currently rank on Google Maps across your service area — not just at your address, but across every neighborhood.

  • One heatmap per keyword you choose to audit
  • Your average ranking across the entire geographic grid
  • Multiplier showing how much more business ranking #1 would bring
  • Visual map overlay of your exact area
06

AI-Written Summary & Optimization Suggestions

Our AI writes a plain-English summary of everything found on your profile — what's costing you rankings, and exactly what to do about it. You also get specific content suggestions so you can start fixing things immediately.

  • Personalized plain-English audit summary
  • Specific fixes prioritized by ranking impact
  • Guidance on services to add and how to describe them
07

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization Report

Your audit scores how well your content performs in answer engines — feature snippets, voice search, "People Also Ask" boxes, and direct answers. These are the zero-click results that capture 50%+ of search traffic.

  • Question match rate & answer clarity score
  • Featured snippet & voice search readiness
  • FAQ structured data & PAA coverage analysis
08

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization Report

See how well your business entity is understood by AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. Your GEO score measures entity clarity, context authority, and AI recommendation readiness.

  • Entity clarity & Knowledge Graph presence
  • LLM recommendation & contextual authority scores
  • Structured entity data & citation consistency
09

Smart Review Platform — Only 4 & 5 Stars Go Public

Our built-in review management system automatically publishes 4 & 5-star reviews to your platforms while routing 1-3 star reviews to your manager for private resolution. Never let a bad review hurt your rating again.

  • 4 & 5-star reviews published automatically
  • 1-3 star reviews held for manager review
  • Full manager dashboard to respond & resolve
